Frequently Asked Questions

Chhote sawaal, seedhe jawab.

Kitne websites support hote hain?+

1,600+ popular platforms (YouTube, Instagram, TikTok, Facebook, X, Vimeo, Reddit, Pinterest, Twitch, etc.) ke saath saath generic extractor ki wajah se almost har woh website bhi support hoti hai jahan public/direct video media maujood hai. Isko yt-dlp engine chalaata hai.

Kya main 4K mein download kar sakta hoon?+

Haan! Jab tak source video 4K/8K available karti hai, aap 4K (2160p) ya usse upar bhi download kar sakte ho. Chhote devices ke liye 144p tak bhi option milta hai.

Kya yeh app 100% free hai?+

Haan, bilkul free. Na sign-up, na pese, na limits. Sirf apna URL paste karo aur download karo.

Kya main sirf audio download kar sakta hoon?+

Haan. "🎵 Audio" quality chuno — app audio-only format download karega. File original format (m4a/webm) me save hoti hai jo har music player me bajti hai.

Private / copyrighted videos par kya?+

Sirf public aur freely available content download hota hai. Private, age-restricted ya login-required content nahi. Download sirf un content ke liye karo jinke malik ho ya use karne ki permission ho. Copyright ka dhyaan rakho.

Instagram fail / Facebook sirf 360p kyun?+

Facebook public videos (SD+HD, exact quality) bina login chalti hain. Instagram ka login-wall sakht hai — website par admin ek baar server cookies dalega to sab users bina-login download karenge; Android APK me 🌐 Server button me apna server URL dalo (best — kuch nahi karna padega) ya IG Login se ek baar apne account se login karo. Private videos me hamesha login lagega.

Preview me 10s skip / Next video kaise?+

Preview ke neeche ⏪ 10s / 10s ⏩ dabao — aage-peeche skip karo. Har video ke neeche Up Next me Related videos dikhte hain (single video par bhi) — tap karo ya Prev/Next dabao, bina link copy agla video khul jayega. Playlist/Channel link ho to uski poori list aati hai. Recent videos bhi tap karke dobara khul jati hain.

Download button dabane par kya hoga?+

Download seedha aapke phone ke Download Manager me start hota hai — 📲 notification bar me % progress aur speed dikhti hai (bilkul VidMate jaisi), wahan se pause/resume bhi kar sakte ho. ★1-Tap quality ek hi file me audio-sahit milti hai (kabhi fail nahi). 1-Tap HD (1080p/4K) ek hi press me video+audio jod kar deta hai — merge fail ho to app khud 1-Tap quality de deta hai.
